# Compliance and launch notes

This project is a software prototype, not a license to operate a betting or financial service. Before launch, obtain independent legal advice for the target country and document:

- business registration and tax obligations;
- advertising and consumer-protection requirements;
- gambling/sports-wagering licensing, if the product involves wagering or odds;
- age, location, identity, AML, responsible-play, refund, and complaint procedures where required;
- data protection, retention, and breach-response procedures;
- payment-provider approval for the exact product and customer journey.

The repository's checkout remains sandbox-only until those checks are complete and a licensed provider is integrated with verified webhooks.
